Our free apps

VaultForge, ThermoForge and our other released apps are free to use. Each app comes with its own licence, which you'll find with its download or source code. That licence decides how you may use, copy and change the app.

  • The free apps are provided as is, without a warranty, to the extent the law allows.
  • You're responsible for your own backups. VaultForge can't recover a vault if the master password is lost. We never have a copy of it.

To the extent the law allows, we aren't liable for indirect losses, lost data or lost profits caused by using the website or the free apps. Nothing on this page limits a liability that the law doesn't allow us to limit.

Custom projects

Business apps that we build from zero or adapt from a Forge app are paid projects. They are not free and not sold as a subscription.

  • Price: each project gets a written, one-time quote before work starts.
  • Agreement: scope, delivery dates, payment terms, ownership of the result, warranty and support are set out in the signed quote or agreement. Where it differs from this page, the agreement wins.
  • Confidentiality: we keep the information you share about your business confidential and use it only for your project.

Privacy policy

In our apps

Our apps process your data on your own computer. They don't send your files, passwords or readings to us. VaultForge stores your vault as an encrypted file on your machine. ThermoForge reads temperatures and process details locally. Its "search online" option opens your web browser, and only when you use it.

On this website

  • Hosting: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address to deliver and secure the site. See the GitHub privacy statement.
  • Fonts: fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address. See the Google privacy policy.
  • Contact form: the name, email, company and message you send pass through Formspree to our inbox. We use them only to answer you and, if you hire us, to run your project. We keep them for [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 24 months], then delete them.

We don't sell your data, and we don't use it for advertising.

Cookies
